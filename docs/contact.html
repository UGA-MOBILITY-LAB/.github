We’re recruiting
Ph.D. students.

Help shape the future of autonomous mobility.

We welcome prospective Ph.D. students interested in autonomous driving, robotics, connected vehicles, and intelligent transportation systems. Backgrounds that fit our work include deep learning, computer vision, hardware acceleration, V2X communication, 3D scene modeling, and field experiments.

Research you could join

  • Robust driving
  • Onboard perception
  • V2X perception
  • 3D scenes & robots
  • Vehicle behavior
  • Rural roads

UGA students looking for research or capstone experience are welcome to get in touch too.

How to apply

  1. Introduce yourself. Email Dr. Yao your CV and a short note on your research interests and the thread that fits you best.
  2. Apply to UGA. Apply to a College of Engineering Ph.D. program through the UGA Graduate School, and name Dr. Yao in your statement of purpose.Graduate admissions ↗Apply online ↗
  3. Ask about funding. Graduate assistantships are awarded separately from admission, so ask about current openings when you write.Assistantship openings ↗
