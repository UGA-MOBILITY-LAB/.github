01

Robust driving

Plans that hold up when cameras degrade.

Haohua Que

DriveDegrade

We benchmark how camera corruption affects camera-only, end-to-end driving and find where planning starts to break down. Closed-loop tests then show how much degradation a driving policy can tolerate.

  • End-to-end planning
  • Camera-corruption benchmarks
  • Closed-loop testing
  • DriveDegrade WACV 2027
  • Camera degradation TRB 2027

02

Efficient onboard perception

Perception that fits on the vehicle.

Haohua Que

FALCON

Vehicles have limited power and strict deadlines. We run camera-based occupancy networks on FPGAs with latency that can be certified, and cut the energy of neural perception with motion-aware approximate memory.

  • FPGA acceleration
  • Latency certification
  • Approximate memory
  • FALCON WACV 2027
  • MotiMem WCCI 2026
2 papers in this thread ↗
03

Cooperative V2X perception

Vehicles that see beyond themselves.

Tianle Zhu · Haohua Que · Qianyi Wu

DinoLink

Vehicles and infrastructure can share what they see over V2X, but bandwidth is limited. We compress shared perception into compact tokens and use cloud-assisted encoding with large multimodal models to keep collaborative perception efficient.

  • Token compression
  • LMM-based encoding
  • Cloud–edge collaboration
  • DinoLink IROS 2026
  • CABLE SMC 2026
2 papers in this thread ↗
04

3D scenes & robot exploration

Understanding places. Navigating possibilities.

Haohua Que · Tianle Zhu

ACESplat · IROS 2026

We regress 3D Gaussian scenes from RGB images and poses alone, localize and render large-scale scenes with mixture-of-experts coordinate encoding, and help robots explore and navigate by predicting the scene ahead.

  • 3D Gaussian splatting
  • Predictive exploration
  • Scene-coordinate localization
  • ACESplat IROS 2026
  • SenseExpo IROS 2026
  • MACE ICRA 2026
3 papers in this thread ↗
05

Vehicle behavior in traffic

How automated and electric vehicles really drive.

Tianle Zhu · Qianyi Wu · Richard Fritz

EV experiments · GA-72© OpenStreetMap contributors

Field experiments and open datasets show how electric vehicles with one-pedal driving follow other cars, and how driver-assistance and automated driving systems interact with traffic lights and signs. We also study edge-computing operations for automated vehicles at stop-controlled intersections.

  • Field experiments
  • Car-following models
  • Interaction datasets
  • Edge computing
  • Network simulation
  • One-pedal EVs CTR 2026
  • One-pedal field tests TRB 2026
  • ADAS & signs TRB 2027
  • ADS & traffic signs TRB 2026
  • AV–signal dataset CTR 2025
  • Edge intersections IET ITS 2025

06

Rural roads, ready for autonomy

Safer roads. Stronger communities.

Tianle Zhu · Haohua Que

Hawkinsville LiDAR & HD map

Through TRAVELS and related projects, we assess whether rural roads are ready for automated vehicles, map Georgia demonstration routes with LiDAR and HD maps, and synthesize the evidence on rural automated-vehicle deployment.

  • LiDAR HD mapping
  • Readiness assessment
  • Community engagement
  • Evidence synthesis
  • Road readiness ICTD 2026
  • Hawkinsville HD map TRAVELS
  • Rural AV evidence hub Open data

01

Tribal & Rural Autonomous Vehicles for Efficiency, Livability and Safety (TRAVELS)

USDOT2026–2032

Connect rural mobility needs with autonomous passenger-service concepts, route assessment, and demonstration planning.

UGA contributes the Georgia site work within the UW–Madison-led TRAVELS program, including local engagement and high-definition mapping.

  • Pulaski County stakeholder visit and mapping data collection in July 2026.
  • Public Hawkinsville route viewer with a Lanelet2 map, LiDAR point cloud, and infrastructure challenge annotations.
  • Candidate service routes for everyday trips, healthcare access, and community recreation.

Rural Road Infrastructure Assessment and Enhancement for Autonomous Vehicle Deployment

USDOT CR2C22025–2026

Assess how rural road markings, signs, and digital connectivity support vehicles with driver assistance and automated driving systems.

A joint North Carolina A&T–UGA project led by Tesfamichael Getahun, with Handong Yao and Qianwen Li as investigators.

  • Camera, GPS, and 5G measurements planned across rural roads and test facilities in Georgia and North Carolina.
  • Planned infrastructure readiness reports and visualizations to help prioritize improvements.
  • UGA study on commercial low-level autonomous vehicles accepted at ASCE ICTD 2026.
