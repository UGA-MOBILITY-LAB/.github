Oct 27, 2026Media coverage

NHTSA Deputy Administrator visits UGA's Future Mobility Living Lab

UGA researchers briefed Lee White, Deputy Administrator of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), on a USDOT-funded rural autonomous vehicle project. Handong Yao, Qianwen (Cami) Li, Haijian Sun, and the Future Mobility Living Lab team presented their work and demonstrated an autonomous vehicle. The Georgia Network for Electric Mobility published video and photo highlights of the visit on October 27, 2026.

Jul 13, 2026Field visit

Pulaski County field visit advances rural AV planning

Handong Yao's UGA team met with local officials in Pulaski County to discuss a demonstration route connecting residential areas with the regional hospital. The visit also included data collection for high-definition mapping.

Jun 30, 2026Publication

One-pedal driving study published in CTR

Our field-experiment study, Investigating the car-following behavior of electric vehicles with one-pedal driving, is published open access in Communications in Transportation Research. It examines how pedal mode interacts with manual driving and adaptive cruise control.

EV platoon dataset published on ETS-Data

Our EV platoon dataset is available through ETS-Data, with synchronized 10 Hz vehicle trajectories collected under one-pedal and two-pedal driving, manual control, and adaptive cruise control.
