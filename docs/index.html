Our perspective

From how vehicles see
to how communities move.

Mobility is more than a vehicle. It is a connected system.

At the University of Georgia, we study autonomous driving, robotics, and transportation infrastructure together. Our research connects perception, planning, and control with the demands of real roads and communities.

Hawkinsville HD map and point-cloud data released

Our rural AV route viewer brings together a Lanelet2 HD map and LiDAR point cloud from Hawkinsville, Georgia. The public map-data release supports interactive exploration of the route and its infrastructure challenges.

Pulaski County field visit advances rural AV planning

Handong Yao's UGA team met with local officials in Pulaski County to discuss a demonstration route connecting residential areas with the regional hospital. The visit also included data collection for high-definition mapping.
