Our perspective

From how vehicles see
to how communities move.

Mobility is more than a vehicle. It is a connected system.

At the University of Georgia, we study autonomous driving, robotics, and transportation infrastructure together. Our research connects perception, planning, and control with the demands of real roads and communities.
