Take the wheel

Can you out-drive
the autopilot?

Drive through towns, farmland, a river bridge, downtown, and pine forest. Stop for red lights and people crossing, pass cyclists and slow farm traffic, and watch for faster cars behind as rain, fog, and nightfall shrink what the sensors can see. Switch on autopilot to let it drive, and take the wheel when it needs you.
